Hassle-Free Returns

Return & Exchange Policy

Our customer-first return process designed for 100% peace of mind.

At Grookfit Apparel, your satisfaction is our highest priority. We want you to love your t-shirts as much as we loved crafting them. To ensure a stress-free shopping experience, we offer a straightforward 7-day return and exchange policy starting from the exact date of package delivery.

If your item arrives with size inaccuracies, print misalignments, or transit damage, our logistics partners will schedule a free reverse pickup directly from your doorstep without any hidden charges.

STEP 1
Submit Request

Contact support via email or WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery.

STEP 2
Doorstep Pickup

Our courier partner collects the unworn item with original tags intact.

STEP 3
Instant Refund

Replacement shipped or full refund credited within 3-5 days.

Eligibility Conditions

  • Apparel must be unworn, unwashed, odor-free, and retain all original tags and bio-bag packaging.
  • Items created inside the Custom Design Studio are printed on-demand specifically for you and are eligible for return only in cases of defect or transit damage.
  • Refunds for Prepaid orders are returned to the source payment method, while Cash on Delivery (COD) orders are refunded via direct UPI / Bank Transfer.
