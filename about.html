About Grookfit Apparel

Empowering self-expression through premium streetwear, custom prints, and cultural apparel.

Established with an unyielding commitment to premium craftsmanship and youth culture, Grookfit Apparel has grown into one of India’s premier destinations for contemporary streetwear, high-density graphic t-shirts, devotional collections, and cinematic merchandise.

At Grookfit, we believe that clothing is far more than mere fabric—it is a bold medium of individual storytelling and cultural identity. From the energy of urban hip-hop and anime fandoms to traditional devotional graphics and cinematic Telugu pop culture, every thread we craft is engineered to represent who you truly are.

Our apparel is built using 100% super-combed bio-washed cotton, ensuring unmatched softness, breathability, and shrink-resistance. We utilize state-of-the-art Direct-To-Garment (DTG) and Screen Printing technology to deliver vibrant, fade-resistant prints that survive hundreds of washes without losing their edge.

Beyond our ready-to-wear drops, our flagship Custom Design Studio enables creators, startups, and fashion enthusiasts to turn their personal artwork into wearable reality. Whether you need an individual masterpiece or custom bulk apparel for corporate and college events, Grookfit delivers top-tier quality directly to your doorstep.

Headquartered out of HITEC City, Hyderabad and expanding across Chennai, our logistics network seamlessly powers rapid deliveries across pan-India, backing every order with dedicated customer support and easy 7-day returns.
