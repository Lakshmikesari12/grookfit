Security & Privacy Policy

How Grookfit safeguards your personal information, custom designs, and payment details.

At Grookfit Apparel, safeguarding your privacy and personal data is fundamental to everything we do. We employ enterprise-grade security architecture across our platform to ensure that your shopping, custom canvas uploads, and transaction history remain 100% confidential and secure.

We strictly adhere to national data protection compliance guidelines and never sell, lease, or share your personal contact information with third-party advertising brokers.

How We Safeguard Your Information

1. Information Collection & Usage

We collect necessary shipping details (Name, Address, Phone Number, Email) solely for order delivery, SMS/WhatsApp dispatch tracking, and customer invoice generation. Custom artwork uploaded into our Custom Studio is stored temporarily in encrypted cloud storage exclusively for print production.

2. Payment & Financial Security

Grookfit does not store or process your credit card numbers, CVVs, or NetBanking credentials on our servers. All financial transactions are seamlessly securely processed via RBI-certified payment aggregators (Razorpay, Paytm, Cashfree).

3. Cookies & Session Management

We use essential functional cookies to remember your shopping cart items, size filter selections, and active session state for a frictionless browsing experience.
